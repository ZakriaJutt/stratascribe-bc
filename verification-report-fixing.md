**User mistake followed end to end** (a wrong vote result is approved and exported): MH-06, MH-08 → S-10 outcome control and blocked states, S-11 review table → `packages/rules`, AD-6, AD-12 → `minute_items` outcome fields and CHECKs, immutable `approvals` → recovery by reopening: `minutes_versions.reopened_from`, "Amended" export, metric M-6 → T-3.2.3, T-3.3.1, T-3.3.2 → tests U-1 to U-6, U-10, U-11, U-14, E-2, E-5, H-1. What no test can catch: a manager attesting a declared outcome that the chair did not declare; that is why the managing broker spot-checks one meeting per manager (T-6.2.2).

The three "worst cases" (commercial: recording not authorized; system: cross-building access; user: wrong vote approved) are related in one paragraph in [PRD.md](PRD.md#highest-cost-user-mistake).
